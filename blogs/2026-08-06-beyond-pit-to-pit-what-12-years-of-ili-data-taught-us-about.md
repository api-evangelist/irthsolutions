On a 10-inch line that's been in the ground since 1971, a standard pit-to-pit corrosion growth model predicted a metal loss anomaly would be at 50% wall loss by 2024. The dig came back, revealing a 35% depth. A 15-point miss on a single feature is enough to send a crew out to dig somewhere it didn't need to.
